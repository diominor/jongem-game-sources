Title:
The Unicorn's Trial

Categories:
Desktop / Mobile

Description:
Dash through the skies and soar above the chaotic dogfights in "The Unicorn's Trial"! Control a swift magical unicorn, dodge relentless enemy fire, and slice through wave after wave of hostile fighter jets and colossal bosses.

How to Play:

Move: Move your mouse / Touch and drag.

Dash Attack: Press [X] key or Tap with 2 fingers.

Game Mechanics & Tips:

Dash Chain: Hitting enemies with your Dash resets the dash cooldown instantly and increases your Chain Count. Chain continuous hits to boost your mobility and explosion power!

Rainbow Repairs: Gaps will appear in the rainbow layer in the background. Dash through or stay over the broken rainbow line to repair it and restore your LIVES (+1)!

Boss Battles: Face off against powerful, heavy-armored bosses as your altitude rises.

Features:

Pure JavaScript & Canvas API (No external assets/libraries).

Procedural Web Audio API sound synthesis & dynamic BGM.

Pixel-art graphics rendered via custom code.
